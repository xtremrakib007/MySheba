MySheba Air Ticket

Flight inquiries handled by a verified dealer who books it and confirms back to you.

MySheba is an air ticket booking app for migrant workers in Malaysia — submit a flight ticket request for routes like Malaysia to Bangladesh, Malaysia to Kathmandu or Malaysia to Manila, and a verified dealer books your plane ticket home.

How Air Ticket Booking Works

MySheba does not operate as a travel agency or Online Travel Agency (OTA). Instead, we connect you to verified travel dealers who handle flight inquiries and bookings on your behalf. You submit your travel details in the app, a dealer reviews availability, books the ticket, and confirms back to you.

Supported Destinations

Flight booking is available for routes connecting Malaysia to supported home countries, including:

🇧🇩 Bangladesh (DAC, CGP) 🇮🇳 India (MAA, CCU, BLR, DEL) 🇳🇵 Nepal (KTM) 🇮🇩 Indonesia (CGK, DPS) 🇵🇰 Pakistan (KHI, LHE, ISB) 🇲🇲 Myanmar (RGN) 🇵🇭 Philippines (MNL, CEB) 🇰🇭 Cambodia (PNH)

Specific routes and availability depend on the dealer network and airline partnerships at the time of request.

Cancellation & Refund

  • Cancellation policies vary by airline and fare type. The dealer will communicate the specific policy before booking.
  • Refund eligibility and timelines depend on the airline's terms; MySheba facilitates but does not control airline refund decisions.
  • Service fees charged by the dealer may be non-refundable even if the airfare is refunded.
