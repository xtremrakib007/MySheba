Complete MySheba App Guide

Guide · Published July 12, 2026

MySheba brings together the everyday services migrant workers in Malaysia need most, in one app. Here's a tour of what's inside.

Money & connectivity

  • Mobile recharge across eight countries, with live rates shown up front.
  • Remittance through a network of verified, licensed dealers, trackable end to end.
  • Mobile banking transfers and internet/data package purchases.

Travel

  • Air ticket booking via dealer-handled flight inquiries.
  • Bus ticket booking through redBus, Bus Online Ticket, or Easybook.
  • Train ticket booking for KTM Intercity and cross-border routes.

Life admin

  • FOMEMA services and visa & passport assistance.
  • Malaysia Arrival Card support.
  • Salary & payslip tools and a secure document vault.

Community

  • Support for account and service assistance.

Getting started

Download the app, verify your number by OTP, and set up a PIN or biometric lock. From there, every service above is one tab away.
