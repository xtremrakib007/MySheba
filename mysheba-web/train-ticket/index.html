MySheba Train Ticket

Train inquiries handled by a verified dealer who books it and confirms back to you.

MySheba is an online train ticket app for migrant workers in Malaysia — book ETS and KTM tickets for routes across Malaysia, and submit Bangladesh Railway ticket inquiries from Malaysia through a verified dealer.

Train Booking Information

MySheba connects you to verified travel dealers who handle train ticket inquiries and bookings. This includes KTM Intercity services in Malaysia and cross-border train routes where available.

Supported Routes

Train booking coverage includes major KTM Intercity routes in Peninsular Malaysia. Specific routes depend on dealer availability and KTM's schedule:

  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Singapore (via KTM Shuttle Tebrau)
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Penang (Butterworth)
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Kota Bharu (Wakaf Bharu)
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Johor Bahru
  • East Coast Line services

Route availability is subject to KTM schedules and may be limited during peak seasons.

Booking Process

1

Submit Inquiry

Enter route, date, class preference, and passenger details.

2

Dealer Review

A dealer checks KTM availability and fare options.

3

Confirmation

The dealer books the ticket and shares the e-ticket with you.

4

Travel Day

Show your e-ticket at the station. Receive departure reminders in the app.

Refund & Cancellation

  • KTM's cancellation policy applies. The dealer will communicate specific terms before booking.
  • Refund eligibility depends on how far in advance cancellation is requested.
  • Dealer service fees may be non-refundable.

FAQ

Seat selection depends on KTM's availability at the time of booking. You can specify preferences in your inquiry, and the dealer will accommodate where possible.
Train operations are managed by KTM. MySheba and our dealers are not responsible for delays or cancellations caused by the operator. We will, however, keep you informed of any status updates received.
