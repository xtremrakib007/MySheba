MySheba Mobile Recharge

Top up any operator across all eight supported countries, with live conversion rates shown before you confirm.

MySheba is a mobile top-up app built for migrant workers in Malaysia who want to recharge Bangladesh, Nepal or Pakistan SIM cards from abroad — top up Grameenphone, Robi, Airtel and Banglalink from Malaysia in a few taps.

Supported Countries

MySheba mobile recharge works across eight countries. Select your destination country, choose the operator, enter the mobile number, and confirm the amount — all with live exchange rates displayed upfront.

How Recharge Works

1

Select Country

Choose the destination country from the list of eight supported nations.

2

Choose Operator

Pick the mobile operator for the number you want to recharge.

3

Enter Number & Amount

Type the mobile number and select the recharge amount. Live rates shown.

4

Confirm & Track

A verified dealer processes your request. Track status in real time.

Internet Packages

In addition to airtime top-up, MySheba also supports data bundle purchases for family and friends abroad. Data bundles are priced and delivered the same way as a regular recharge — select the country, operator, package type, and confirm.

Important Terms

  • Recharge requests are processed by verified dealers within the MySheba network.
  • Live exchange rates are displayed before confirmation but may fluctuate slightly during processing.
  • Processing times vary by operator and country, typically between a few minutes to one hour.
  • You must have sufficient wallet balance before submitting a recharge request.
  • MySheba does not guarantee instant delivery for all operators; dealer acceptance is required.
