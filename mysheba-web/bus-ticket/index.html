MySheba Bus Ticket

Choose a ticketing partner to continue. 0 pts deducted only after your payment is successful.

MySheba is a bus ticket booking app for Malaysia built with foreign workers in mind — book an online interstate bus ticket from Kuala Lumpur or anywhere in Malaysia through trusted ticketing partners, right from the app.

Ticketing Partners

MySheba integrates with established bus ticketing platforms to provide you with options. You select your preferred partner in the app, and the booking is handled through their platform:

🚌 redBus Ticket 🚌 Bus Online Ticket 🚌 Easybook

Supported Routes

Bus ticket booking primarily covers routes within Malaysia and cross-border services to Singapore and Thailand. Specific operators and routes depend on the partner platform selected:

  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Singapore
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Penang
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Johor Bahru
  • Kuala Lumpur ↔ Kota Bharu
  • Inter-city routes within Peninsular Malaysia

Route availability is determined by the partner platform and may change without notice.

Booking Process

1

Select Partner

Choose redBus, Bus Online Ticket, or Easybook from the app.

2

Search Route

Enter origin, destination, and travel date to see available buses.

3

Choose & Pay

Select your seat and bus operator. Pay using your MySheba wallet.

4

Get Ticket

Receive your e-ticket in the app and via confirmation.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellation and refund policies are set by the individual ticketing partner and bus operator.
  • MySheba facilitates the transaction but does not control partner refund decisions.
  • Points deducted from your wallet are refunded only if the partner confirms cancellation eligibility.
  • Always review the partner's terms before confirming a booking.

FAQ

Each partner offers different operators and routes. We recommend comparing options across partners in the app to find the best schedule and price for your route.
Cancellation requests are forwarded to the partner platform. Approval and refund timing depend on the partner's policy and the bus operator's terms.
