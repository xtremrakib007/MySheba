About MySheba

Everything you need here. Everyone you love, still close.

Our Mission

MySheba was built for the millions of workers living in Malaysia who support families back home. We believe that sending money, topping up a phone, or booking a ticket should not require standing in line for hours or navigating complex systems in a foreign language.

Our mission is simple: bring every essential service into one app, with real people — verified dealers — handling requests so nothing gets lost in automation.

Who We Serve

MySheba is designed specifically for migrant workers in Malaysia who need to stay connected to Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Indonesia, Pakistan, Myanmar, Philippines, and Cambodia. We understand the challenges of working abroad: language barriers, confusing paperwork, and the anxiety of sending money home safely.

Our Story

MySheba started with a simple observation: workers in Malaysia were using five or more apps, visiting multiple shops, and waiting in long queues just to send money home or top up a relative's phone. We built MySheba to unify these services into one platform, with a dealer network that adds a human layer of trust and accountability.

Legal Notice

MySheba is a technology platform, not a bank or licensed financial institution. Remittance and mobile banking services are facilitated through third-party licensed dealers and partners. We do not hold customer deposits directly. All financial services are subject to the terms of the respective licensed providers.
