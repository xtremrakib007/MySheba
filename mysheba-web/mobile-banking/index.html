MySheba Mobile Banking

Send to a mobile banking account back home and track it from submitted to delivered, with a collection PIN for peace of mind.

Need to send money to bKash from Malaysia, or pay Nagad and Rocket bills back home? MySheba brings mobile banking Bangladesh from abroad into one app, built for foreign workers in Malaysia.

What We Provide

MySheba enables you to send money directly to mobile banking wallets and accounts in supported destination countries. This is not a traditional bank transfer — it is a facilitated transfer through our verified dealer network to mobile financial service providers.

Important: MySheba is not a bank. We are not licensed to accept deposits or operate as a financial institution. Mobile banking transfers are processed through licensed third-party dealers and partners who hold appropriate regulatory approvals in their respective jurisdictions.

Supported Mobile Banking Services

  • Bangladesh — bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay (where dealer-supported)
  • India — Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay (where dealer-supported)
  • Nepal — eSewa, Khalti (where dealer-supported)
  • Other countries — Availability varies by dealer and local mobile money infrastructure.

Wallet & Points

MySheba provides a unified wallet balance across every service. You top up your wallet via bank transfer, and the balance is reviewed and credited before use. One wallet works for recharge, remittance, ticketing, and all other services.

  • Top up by bank transfer to MySheba's designated account.
  • Balance is reviewed and credited before it's available for use.
  • Earn points on eligible transactions that can be redeemed for discounts.
  • Wallet balance is non-transferable between users.

How It Works

  1. Top up your MySheba wallet via bank transfer.
  2. Select "Mobile Banking" and choose the destination country.
  3. Enter the recipient's mobile banking number and account details.
  4. Confirm the amount and review the live exchange rate.
  5. A verified dealer processes the transfer and updates status in real time.
