MySheba Remittance

Check today's rate, then send — with the full request tracked from your side and theirs.

Looking for the best remittance app for foreign workers in Malaysia? MySheba lets you send money home instantly — including bKash, Nagad and Rocket transfers from Malaysia — so migrant workers can send money to Bangladesh, Nepal or Pakistan without visiting an agent.

What MySheba Remittance Provides

MySheba connects workers in Malaysia to their families across eight countries through a verified dealer network. You submit a remittance request in the app, a vetted dealer accepts it, processes the transfer through their licensed channels, and updates the status in real time.

MySheba itself is not a bank or licensed money services operator. Remittance services are facilitated through third-party licensed dealers and partners. MySheba provides the technology platform that connects you to these providers.

🇧🇩 Bangladesh 🇮🇳 India 🇳🇵 Nepal 🇮🇩 Indonesia 🇵🇰 Pakistan 🇲🇲 Myanmar 🇵🇭 Philippines 🇰🇭 Cambodia

Supported Channels

  • Mobile banking deposit — Send directly to a mobile banking account back home (bKash, Nagad, etc. where supported).
  • Bank transfer — Deposit to a traditional bank account in the destination country.
  • Cash pickup — Collect cash from designated pickup points (availability varies by country).
  • Home delivery — Available in select locations through partner networks.

Fees & Limits

Fees and transfer limits vary by destination country, channel, and dealer. The exact fee and exchange rate are displayed in the app before you confirm any request. There are no hidden charges — what you see at confirmation is what you pay.

  • Minimum and maximum limits are set per transaction and per day.
  • Exchange rates are live and may fluctuate slightly during processing.
  • Dealer commission is included in the displayed rate.
